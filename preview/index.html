About Me

Grace Mussimbi

IT Support & Help Desk

IT support professional. CompTIA Security+ certified, fluent in French and English, with four years of technical work in ticket-driven environments and front-line customer support.

I build, configure and document Windows Server and Active Directory infrastructure in my own lab, and I diagnose faults the way they arrive at a service desk: from the reported symptom, through the evidence, to a verified fix. Before that I spent four years resolving technical issues for clients and internal teams, working requests through Jira and help desk queues, debugging across browsers, platforms and data sources, and writing up what was found.

Every writeup on this site follows the same structure: configuration, method, verification and evidence, including the failures and what resolved them.
